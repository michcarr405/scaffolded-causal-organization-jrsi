# Result freeze — Abstract lattice fidelity × time surface v1.0.0

## Classification
**FROZEN POST-CONFIRMATORY MECHANISTIC MAPPING RESULT**

This freeze promotes `ABSTRACT_LATTICE_FIDELITY_TIME_SURFACE_DIAGNOSTIC_v1.0.0` from a completed diagnostic campaign to a frozen mechanistic-mapping result. It does not alter the classification of the earlier `ABSTRACT_LATTICE_CONFIRMATORY_v1.0.0`, which remains the canonical prospective existence test for the four qualitative signatures.

## Frozen design
- 30 entirely new root-seed blocks for this mapping campaign.
- 41 transmission fidelities: `p=0,0.025,...,0.975,1.0`.
- Selected and matched-neutral trajectories recorded every generation `t=0..50`.
- Full inherited populations archived every generation in bit-packed state files.
- Six sentinel fidelities `p={0.50,0.70,0.80,0.90,0.95,1.00}` continued to generation 200.
- History-specificity, acquisition-expression, and equal-distance anisotropy probes at `t={0,5,10,15,20,30,40,50}`.
- Model architecture and parameters unchanged from the confirmed abstract-lattice control.
- Plan, configuration, seed ledger, production code, and state decoder SHA256-frozen before outcomes.

## M1 — Fidelity-response geometry
**Frozen result:** within this abstract realization, the observed fidelity dependence is better described by a smooth increase in stable relational causal value than by an abrupt threshold among the three prespecified candidate shape models.

Define `A50(p)` as mean `DeltaV_rel` over generations 41–50. Representative values are:

| p | A50 |
|---:|---:|
| 0.50 | 0.02931 |
| 0.70 | 0.06114 |
| 0.80 | 0.09395 |
| 0.90 | 0.13720 |
| 0.95 | 0.15934 |
| 0.975 | 0.17255 |
| 1.00 | 0.17952 |

Prespecified descriptive shape comparison on the 41-point mean curve:
- logistic: `AICc=-497.634`, best model;
- continuous segmented linear: `Delta AICc=44.742`;
- hard two-plateau step: `Delta AICc=198.506`.

The logistic was the lowest-AICc model in **1000/1000 root-block bootstrap resamples**.

### Boundary
The fitted logistic midpoint hit the physical boundary `p=1`; its implied upper saturation lies outside the allowed fidelity domain. Therefore a continuously estimated critical fidelity or asymptotic `p50` is **not identified**.

Post-outcome descriptive observed-range landmarks may be reported only as such:
- `p10_observed ~= 0.375`;
- `p50_observed ~= 0.788`;
- `p90_observed ~= 0.954`.

These are not phase-transition points.

## M2 — Fidelity changes both stable amplitude and relative acquisition timescale
**Frozen result:** fidelity changes both the stable magnitude of inherited relational causal value and the relative timescale over which that larger magnitude is assembled; the dominant effect across the tested domain is the increase in stable amplitude.

Empirical onset landmarks for `DeltaV_rel` include:

| p | t50 | t90 |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.50 | 1 | 4 |
| 0.70 | 2 | 5 |
| 0.80 | 3 | 7 |
| 0.90 | 3 | 10 |
| 0.95 | 4 | 10 |
| 0.975 | 4 | 12 |
| 1.00 | 6 | 21 |

Thus very high fidelity supports a much larger stable effect while taking more generations to approach that larger plateau in relative terms. Exponential fits are secondary support because low-amplitude block fits are often unidentifiable; where >=20 high-fidelity block fits are identifiable, fitted `k` declines strongly with fidelity (`Spearman rho ~= -0.979`).

### Boundary
This result does not establish a universal rate-amplitude law, nor that increasing fidelity must slow acquisition in every realization.

## M4 — Relational causal value and fitness differentiation emerge nearly together in this abstract control
**Frozen result:** across the dense block-mean fidelity × time surface, `DeltaV_rel` and selected-neutral fitness differentiation `DeltaF` are nearly identical (`Pearson r=0.9999896`) and their empirical relative onset times are closely matched.

This abstract lattice therefore does **not** show relational causal organization clearly preceding measurable fitness differentiation.

### General-theory implication
Temporal precedence of relational causal organization over fitness differentiation is not a mathematical necessity of the scaffolded-causal architecture; it can be realization-dependent.

## M5 — Higher-order signatures have distinct temporal profiles
At `p=0.95`, the prespecified probes reveal different dynamics:

### History specificity
Mean matching-history fitness advantage:
- `t=5`: `0.01929`;
- `t=10`: `0.02445` (transient maximum among sampled times);
- `t=40`: `0.00955`;
- `t=50`: `0.00966`.

History specificity therefore emerges rapidly, peaks transiently, and relaxes to a smaller stable effect.

### Acquisition-expression separation
Frozen-signature contrast:
- `t=5`: `0.05273`;
- `t=10`: `0.08215`;
- `t=15`: `0.09819`;
- `t=30`: `0.10504`;
- `t=50`: `0.10556`.

### Equal-distance anisotropy
Frozen-signature contrast:
- `t=5`: `0.07999`;
- `t=10`: `0.12591`;
- `t=15`: `0.14814`;
- `t=40`: `0.15639`;
- `t=50`: `0.15634`.

Acquisition-expression separation and anisotropy rise toward stable plateaus, whereas history specificity is most pronounced transiently.

### Boundary
The exact temporal shapes are mechanistic mapping results, not independently preregistered qualitative predictions distinct from the already-confirmed existence of these signatures.

## Secondary descriptive result — structural correlates
At generation 50 across fidelity, larger `DeltaV_rel` covaries with greater selected-neutral task projection, stronger nearest-neighbor order, lower heterozygosity, and systematic scaffold projection. These are **secondary descriptive correlates**, not independent causal tests, because fidelity jointly drives the variables.

## Canonical interpretation
The new mapping resolves the ambiguous use of “threshold” for the abstract lattice. The data support a **smooth fidelity-dependent increase in stable inherited relational causal capacity across the tested domain**, with no observed saturation before the physical upper boundary. Very high fidelity supports larger stable organization but a slower relative approach to that larger plateau.

This is a mechanistic map of an already-confirmed theoretical control. It does not establish a universal critical fidelity, thermodynamic phase transition, universality, chemical realism, scaffold endogenization, autonomous agency, or full Walker-Davies reversal.

## Relationship to the older freeze
The earlier lattice confirmation remains canonical for the existence of the four prospectively tested qualitative signatures. The present campaign supersedes the older campaign only for higher-resolution descriptive estimates and visualization of fidelity and temporal structure.

**New data supersede old estimates where resolution improved; they do not supersede the old confirmation.**
