# ABSTRACT_LATTICE_FIDELITY_TIME_SURFACE_RESULT_FREEZE_v1.0.0

## Classification
**FROZEN POST-CONFIRMATORY MECHANISTIC MAPPING RESULT**

This package freezes the dense abstract-lattice fidelity × time mapping campaign as a mechanistic result. It does **not** replace, rewrite, or absorb the earlier `ABSTRACT_LATTICE_CONFIRMATORY_v1.0.0` evidence record.

The evidence hierarchy is intentionally:

`discovery -> prospective confirmation -> independent post-confirmatory mechanistic mapping`.

The older confirmatory campaign remains the canonical evidence that four qualitative architecture-level signatures were independently prospectively confirmed:

1. scaffold-specific acquisition;
2. transmission-fidelity dependence;
3. acquisition-expression separation;
4. equal-distance anisotropic retrieval.

The dense mapping campaign refines the mechanism by resolving:

- `DeltaV_rel = f(p,t)` over 41 fidelity values and every generation 0–50;
- the shape of the fidelity-response curve;
- acquisition timescales;
- generation-50 versus generation-200 stability;
- transient history specificity;
- time-dependent acquisition-expression separation and anisotropy;
- the near-coincident dynamics of `DeltaV_rel` and selected-neutral fitness differentiation in this abstract control.

## Canonical rule
**New data supersede old descriptive estimates and visualizations where resolution improved; they do not supersede the old prospective confirmation.**

## Freeze boundary
No additional runs, seeds, fidelity values, generations, estimators, or newly derived claims are to be inserted into this v1.0.0 result freeze. Analyses performed later from the archived full inherited states must receive a new diagnostic or result version.
