# Manuscript use guide

## Main rule
Use the **new dense mapping data for the best descriptive plots and estimates**, while retaining the **older frozen confirmatory campaign as the canonical independent prospective existence test**.

## Recommended manuscript replacements
- Replace the old sparse fidelity curve with the new 41-point fidelity curve or `p x t` surface.
- Replace coarse temporal trajectories with every-generation trajectories where temporal dynamics are discussed.
- Replace “threshold-like” wording for the abstract lattice with “smooth fidelity-dependent amplification/storage capacity across the tested range.”
- Replace endpoint-only timing language with empirical `t50/t90` and sentinel stability results when discussing mechanism.

These are improvements in resolution and description, not replacements of the evidentiary record.

## Recommended wording
### Existence claim
> An independent prospective lattice confirmation established scaffold-specific acquisition, heredity dependence, acquisition-expression separation, and equal-distance anisotropy on fresh root-seed blocks.

### Mapping claim
> A subsequent independently seeded post-confirmatory mapping campaign resolved these effects across 41 transmission fidelities and every generation through 50, with selected sentinel trajectories continued to generation 200.

### Fidelity-shape claim
> In the abstract lattice, stable relational causal value increased smoothly across the tested fidelity range; among three prespecified descriptive candidate models, a smooth logistic outperformed segmented and hard-step alternatives, while saturation was not identified within the physical fidelity domain.

### Timing claim
> Fidelity affected both stable amplitude and relative acquisition timescale: higher fidelity supported substantially larger stable relational causal value, while the relative approach to that larger plateau extended over more generations near perfect transmission.

## Avoid
Do not write:
- “the old lattice confirmation was replaced by the dense campaign”;
- “a universal critical fidelity was ruled out”;
- “there is no threshold” without the qualifier “among the tested descriptive candidate models / within this abstract realization”;
- “p=0.788 is the critical fidelity”;
- “all dynamics are complete by generation 50”;
- “the mapping campaign independently reconfirmed all four signatures” as its principal evidentiary classification.

## Figure-level recommendation
For the main-text abstract-control panel, prefer the newer high-resolution visual summaries. In the caption or provenance text, state that the four qualitative signatures were previously established in the independent prospective confirmatory campaign and that the displayed dense data come from a subsequent mechanistic mapping campaign.
