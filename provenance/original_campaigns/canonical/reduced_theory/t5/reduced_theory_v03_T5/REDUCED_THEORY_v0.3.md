# Reduced Theory v0.3 — Scaffolded Causal Organization as a Relational Response Geometry

## Status
Theory revision after T4 and prospective confirmation by T5. v0.3 retains the v0.2 acquisition-expression result confirmed by T3, but replaces the scalar scaffold-distance law falsified by T4 with a mechanistic anisotropic response geometry. The T5 directional test was frozen before any T5 evolutionary or cross-evaluation outcomes and passed all three primary criteria.

## 1. Why v0.2 required revision
v0.2 proposed that historical-match loss could be described mainly by a scalar distance from the historical scaffold. T4 showed this is not generally true. At the same Euclidean scaffold displacement, different directions produced substantially different historical-match losses, and the class-mean response became nonmonotonic at large displacement.

The central correction is that the scaffold is not just a scalar strength or location in an isotropic space. A scaffold perturbation changes a motif-dependent chemical kernel, and the consequences depend on how that kernel is contracted with the relational organization that selection built.

## 2. Factorization of inherited organization and physical readout
Let an adjacent inherited motif-context type be

a = (s,m),

where s is physical context/segment and m is motif identity.

For a population X, let W_X(a,b) denote the normalized abundance of adjacent inherited pairs a -> b. This is an inherited relational statistic: it records not only which motifs are present, but how motif identities are arranged across adjacent physical contexts.

A current scaffold B defines the local chemical state kernel

K_a(z;B) = P(z | s,m,B).

In Model B this kernel is the fixed softmax of motif affinity plus scaffold bias.

Let R(z,z') be the fixed pairwise chemical payoff matrix,

R = reward * I_productive - penalty * I_anti.

Before the fitness floor is applied, the expected fitness of a frozen population is

F_raw(X,B) = 1 + sum_{a,b} W_X(a,b) K_a(B)^T R K_b(B).

For paired selected and neutral histories define

Delta W(a,b) = W_S(a,b) - W_N(a,b).

Then the selected-neutral lineage advantage under any current scaffold is

D(B) = F_raw(S,B) - F_raw(N,B)
     = sum_{a,b} Delta W(a,b) K_a(B)^T R K_b(B).

This is the central v0.3 equation.

It cleanly separates:

- **what evolution stores:** Delta W, the inherited relational organization;
- **how current physics reads it:** K(B), the scaffold-dependent chemical kernel;
- **how chemistry maps to reproductive consequence:** R, the fixed pair payoff map.

Thus the causal/evolutionary effect is neither contained in the scaffold alone nor in inherited organization alone. It is generated by their contraction through fixed microscopic physics.

## 3. Historical-match loss
Let B_H be the historical scaffold. Define

Delta H(B) = D(B_H) - D(B).

Under the scalar theory, Delta H was assumed to depend predominantly on ||B-B_H||^2. v0.3 predicts instead that equal Euclidean displacements can have different effects because different directions change K(B) differently relative to Delta W and R.

This is a **response geometry**, not necessarily a mathematical metric. The historical scaffold need not be a stationary local maximum of D in an enlarged continuous scaffold space.

## 4. Local anisotropic expansion
Vectorize the scaffold field as b and let delta = b-b_H. Around the historical scaffold,

Delta H(delta)
 = -g^T delta + 1/2 delta^T G delta + O(||delta||^3),

where

g = grad D(b_H)

and

G = - Hessian D(b_H).

The old scalar-distance law is a special case requiring both

g = 0

and

G proportional to the identity on the admissible scaffold subspace.

T4 shows that these assumptions are not generally valid. Direction-specific effects at identical Euclidean distance imply anisotropy, and the near-zero or negative response in some very small directions is consistent with the historical scaffold not being a strict local optimum in the continuous probe space.

At finite displacement the full nonlinear D(B) should replace the quadratic approximation.

## 5. Post-T4 mechanistic diagnostic
Using the 30 already-frozen T4 populations, we evaluated the exact expected **pre-floor** pair-kernel score above for every T4 probe direction. This analysis was performed after seeing T4 and is explanatory, not prospective.

Across the 28 nonhistorical T4 probe scaffolds:

- Euclidean squared scaffold distance alone explained about 50.4% of the variance in mean observed DeltaH.
- A motif-weighted chemical-kernel JSD explained about 70.1%.
- The full pair-kernel contraction predicted the directional profile with Pearson r = 0.9993 and affine R^2 = 0.9986 at the direction-mean level.
- At the pooled root-block x probe level, the raw pair-kernel predictor had r = 0.9969 and affine R^2 = 0.9938.
- Across individual root blocks, median directional correlation was close to 1; the mean blockwise affine R^2 was 0.9973.

The near-unit slope and modest negative intercept arise because the reduced pair-kernel expression is the expectation before the Model B cell-fitness floor at 0.1, whereas the stochastic assay applies the floor after each chemical realization. The directional structure is nevertheless almost completely captured by the inherited-pair x chemical-kernel contraction.

## 6. Prospective T5 test of anisotropy
T5 was frozen before new evolutionary outcomes were generated.

### Design
Thirty entirely new selected-neutral root blocks evolved for 250 generations under the historical SCF0 scaffold. Frozen generation-250 populations were then tested against eight new continuous scaffold directions.

All eight probes were constructed at exactly the same squared Euclidean distance d=64 from the historical scaffold and retained the same per-segment scaffold norm. The directions were 22.5, 67.5, 112.5, 157.5, 202.5, 247.5, 292.5, and 337.5 degrees, none of which were used in T4.

Before T5 evolution, the angular response profile was calibrated from the existing T4 root blocks using the pair-kernel equation and frozen.

### Primary results
All three prespecified primary tests passed.

1. **Directional profile.** Frozen pair-kernel prediction versus observed T5 direction means:

   r = 0.9879,
   exact 8! label-permutation p = 0.000248.

2. **Extremal paired contrast.** The frozen model predicted P13 (292.5 degrees) to exceed P09 (202.5 degrees). It did so in 30/30 independent T5 root blocks:

   one-sided exact sign p = 9.31e-10.

3. **Equal-distance anisotropy.** Direction means differed strongly even though all probes had identical Euclidean distance:

   within-block direction-label permutation p = 2.00e-5.

The secondary frozen affine absolute-value prediction gave

R^2 = 0.9496

and RMSE = 0.5062 fitness units across the eight new direction means.

The exact predicted top direction (P13) was not the observed top direction (P01), although P13 remained second-highest and the complete directional profile was strongly predicted. The predicted weakest direction P09 was observed to be the weakest.

## 7. What T5 establishes
T5 prospectively rejects the idea that historical specificity can be summarized by Euclidean scaffold distance alone.

At a fixed physical displacement magnitude,

Delta H(B_1) != Delta H(B_2)

because the direction of scaffold change determines how the fixed chemical kernel reads the inherited organization.

The relevant geometry is therefore jointly defined by:

1. the evolved relational state Delta W;
2. the motif-dependent physical response kernel K;
3. the chemical fitness/payoff map R.

This is a stronger statement than "the lineage remembers its scaffold." It says that the inherited historical imprint has a **structured domain of applicability**: some changes in physical context preserve or expose its consequences far more than equally large changes in other directions.

## 8. Revised general mechanism
v0.3 now has four stages:

**physical regularity -> selective acquisition -> inherited relational state -> context-dependent readout geometry.**

A stable physical asymmetry supplies a coordinate structure. Selection builds an inherited arrangement relative to that structure. The inherited arrangement is then read through the current physical kernel, so its causal expression depends on both evolutionary history and the direction of present environmental change.

This yields three separable quantities:

- **acquisition:** how much relational organization selection builds;
- **storage:** the inherited relational state itself;
- **retrieval/expression:** how strongly a current physical context makes that state consequential.

T3 prospectively supports the acquisition-expression separation. T5 prospectively supports directional, anisotropic retrieval.

## 9. Broader theoretical interpretation
The result strengthens the scaffolding interpretation of Model B.

The earliest regulatory-like organization need not encode an entire control architecture internally. The physical environment can supply a stable coordinate system, while evolution stores a complementary response in inherited relational organization. What that inherited organization does later depends on how the current physical context projects onto the structure that evolution learned.

In this sense, early control can be distributed across

**inherited relational organization x persistent physical structure**

rather than residing entirely in an autonomous internal controller.

## 10. Limits
- The pair-kernel equation is a mechanistic reduction of Model B, not yet a proof of universality beyond Model B.
- It predicts expected pre-floor fitness; the cell-level floor produces a modest systematic offset.
- The T5 angular profile was calibrated from prior T4 populations, so T5 is prospective out-of-sample evolutionary validation of a calibrated response geometry, not a parameter-free derivation from first principles.
- The framework does not establish scaffold endogenization, autonomous memory after scaffold removal, or formal PhiID causal emergence.
- Generality beyond Model B still requires a substantially different microscopic realization.

## 11. Next highest-value test
The highest-value next step is no longer another Model B parameter sweep. It is an independent microscopic realization that preserves only the abstract ingredients:

1. an externally supplied physical asymmetry;
2. heritable internal degrees of freedom;
3. selection acting on lower-level outcomes;
4. a fixed nonlinear physical readout;
5. an intervention that isolates the causal contribution of evolved relational organization.

The general-theory prediction is that such a system should reproduce:

- scaffolded acquisition of inherited organization;
- stronger acquisition with reliable heredity;
- separation of training conditions from expression-optimal conditions;
- history-specific response;
- anisotropic sensitivity to changes in the physical scaffold.
